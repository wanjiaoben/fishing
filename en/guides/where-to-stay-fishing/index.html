Okinawa fishing trip planning

Where to Stay for Okinawa Fishing

Okinawa is larger than many visitors expect. If your hotel is on the wrong side of the island, a fishing day can start with a one to two hour one-way transfer before sunrise.

Use this guide to match your stay area to the real departure ports used on this site: Chatan, Kadena and Awase.

Ask which port fits your dates →
Simple rule

Stay near the departure side, not just near the airport

Fishing charters leave early. A hotel area that feels convenient for shopping or nightlife can be inconvenient for a 04:00–06:00 meeting time.

For west-coast fishing from Chatan, Chatan Hamagawa or Kadena, choose a west-coast base if you can. For east-coast fishing from Uruma Awase Port, choose an east-side or central area if your schedule allows.

If you are not sure which port your plan will use, send your dates, group size and preferred fishing style before booking accommodation. The captain can advise which side is more practical for that week.

By departure port

Recommended stay areas

Area names only. We do not recommend specific hotels here.

Chatan American Village Port / Chatan Hamagawa Port

Best stay areas for Chatan departure

  • Chatan
  • American Village
  • Ginowan
  • Onna, if you prefer a resort base and accept a longer transfer

These areas are practical for west-coast private charters, including Amberjack Live Bait and many GT, tuna or jigging arrangements when the captain uses a west-coast port.

Kadena Fishing Port

Best stay areas for Kadena shared boat

  • Kadena
  • Chatan
  • Yomitan
  • Onna
  • Ginowan

The shared fishing boat Okinawa experience departs from Kadena Fishing Port, runs about 2 hours and is built for first-timers and families.

Uruma Awase Port

Best stay areas for Awase departure

  • Uruma
  • Awase
  • Okinawa City
  • Chatan, if crossing the island early is acceptable

Awase is on the east side. It can be the better departure side depending on wind, fishing ground and the plan selected by the captain.

Before booking a hotel

Send your dates first if fishing is the priority

For a fishing charter for 2-3 people, the departure port can matter as much as the plan price. Share your dates, group size, fishing experience and where you are thinking of staying.

We will confirm which departure side makes sense, whether the shared boat or private charter route fits your group, and what meeting point to expect.

If fishing is only one day of a longer Okinawa trip, you can still stay where you prefer — just plan the transfer time honestly.

FAQ

Where to stay FAQ

Is Naha a good place to stay for a fishing charter?

Naha can work if you accept an early transfer, but it is not the closest base for Chatan, Kadena or Awase fishing departures. For early morning charters, staying closer to the departure side usually makes the day easier.

Where should I stay for Chatan or Kadena departure?

For Chatan, Chatan Hamagawa or Kadena Fishing Port, convenient areas include Chatan, American Village, Kadena, Yomitan, Onna and Ginowan. These keep the west-coast meeting points easier than staying far south or far north.

Where should I stay for Awase departure?

For Uruma Awase Port, convenient areas include Uruma, Awase and Okinawa City. Chatan can also work if you are comfortable crossing the island early.
