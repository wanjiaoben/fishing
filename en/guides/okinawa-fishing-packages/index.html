all-inclusive per private boat

A private boat fishing plan from Chatan for guests who want the real boat fishing experience without joining strangers. The half-day option is about 4 hours; the full-day option is 7–8 hours.

DurationAbout 4 hours
DepartureAbout 8:30 a.m. or about 1:00 p.m.

FAQ

Fishing package FAQ

How much do these Okinawa fishing packages cost?

Private Boat Fishing from Chatan is JPY 72,000 for a private half-day charter, or JPY 108,000 for a private full-day charter. Kayak/SUP + Fishing from Kadena is JPY 82,000 for the private half-day + snorkeling combination.

How long is the private boat fishing plan and where does it depart?

Private Boat Fishing from Chatan is about 4 hours, with departure around 8:30 a.m. or around 1:00 p.m. The departure port is Chatan. The Kayak/SUP + Fishing combo departs from Kadena.

Can beginners join these fishing packages?

Yes. Beginners are welcome. English booking and WhatsApp support are coordinated with trusted local captains. The Kayak/SUP + Fishing plan is the lighter, casual family-friendly combo.

What's included in the price?

Our price includes a private boat charter with a licensed local captain; rods, reels, tackle and bait; life jackets and safety equipment; and English booking support and WhatsApp coordination before and on the day.

What's not included in the price?

Food and drinks are not included — please bring your own. We keep it this way because of allergies and personal preferences.

What free options can I request?

Free on request: a styrofoam cooler box with ice and basic utensils so you can take your catch home (yours to keep); reservation at a local restaurant that will cook your catch for you (you pay the restaurant directly); and child life jackets and snorkel gear on combo trips. None of the above options carry an extra charge from us.
