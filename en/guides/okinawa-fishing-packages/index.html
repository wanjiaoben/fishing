FAQ

Fishing package FAQ

How much do these Okinawa fishing packages cost?

Private Boat Fishing from Chatan is JPY 76,000 per private boat for up to 6 guests. Kayak/SUP + Fishing from Kadena is Adult JPY 11,550 / Child JPY 9,350; for example, 1 adult + 2 children = JPY 30,250.

How long is the private boat fishing plan and where does it depart?

Private Boat Fishing from Chatan is about 4 hours, with departure around 8:30 a.m. or around 1:00 p.m. The departure port is Chatan. The Kayak/SUP + Fishing combo departs from Kadena.

Can beginners join these fishing packages?

Yes. Beginners are welcome. For the private boat plan, this season the captain recommends trolling. The Kayak/SUP + Fishing plan is the lighter, casual family-friendly combo.
